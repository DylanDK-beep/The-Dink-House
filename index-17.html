Weekly events

Two regular sessions, both open to every skill level. New to pickleball? Start with Rec Play.

Wednesday, 14:00 to 16:00

Rec Play

Relaxed play for all levels. Book a 2-hour session and we will match you with other players. It is the easiest way to meet people and improve your game.

R35for 2 hours
Book Rec Play
Friday, 18:00 to 20:00

Americano

Round robin play where you change partners every round and keep your own score. We finish with a bring and braai, so bring something for the fire.

R50per player
